# Ski Resort Performance Analysis Dashboard – 2022
# 📌Project Overview

This project analyzes ski resort data using Power BI to identify pricing,
seasonal, and resort performance trends. Interactive dashboards and visualizations
were created for better analysis.The analysis helps identify differences in resort distribution, pricing patterns,
and operational performance across countries and continents. It simplifies complex
data into meaningful insights.This project supports data-driven decision-making using interactive charts,
KPI cards, and slicers. It helps understand resort trends, seasonal demand, and
business performance effectively.
   
# 🎯 Project Objectives

The key objectives of this project are:
- To analyze the geographical distribution of ski resorts across different
continents and countries using pie charts and clustered bar chart
visualizations for better regional comparison and tourism analysis.

- To evaluate average resort pricing trends across countries and continents
using clustered column charts and line charts in order to identify premium
tourism regions and pricing variations.

- To compare beginner, intermediate, and difficult slope categories using
stacked chart analysis to understand skiing difficulty distribution and customer
accessibility across resorts.

- To examine the relationship between lift capacity and resort operational
performance using scatter chart visualization for identifying infrastructure
efficiency and visitor management capability.

- To develop an interactive Power BI dashboard using KPI cards, slicers, and
multiple charts for dynamic filtering, real-time analysis, and effective business
performance monitoring.

- To generate meaningful descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive
analytics insights that support data-driven decision-making, tourism planning,
and resort management strategies

# 📈 Key Insights

### Descriptive Analytics (What Happened?)

Europe holds nearly 45% of total ski resorts, showing the highest resort concentration.
Medium-price resorts contribute around 50% of total slopes compared to low and high-price categories.
Resorts with higher lift capacity generally show higher total slope counts in the scatter analysis.
Winter season records the highest resort activity and customer preference across most countries.

### Diagnostic Analytics (Why It Happened?)

Europe dominates due to better snowfall conditions, tourism infrastructure, and established ski culture.
Medium-price resorts attract more visitors because they balance affordability and facilities.
Resorts with greater lift capacity can manage more visitors, increasing operational efficiency.
Seasonal demand rises during winter because weather conditions are most suitable for skiing activities.

### Predictive Analytics (What Will Happen?)

High-capacity resorts are expected to experience increased visitor growth in future seasons.
Medium-price resorts may continue dominating market demand due to budget-friendly pricing.
Countries with growing tourism investments could see a rise in new ski resort development.
Seasonal demand is likely to remain highest during winter periods over the coming years.

### Prescriptive Analytics (What Should Be Done?)

Resort operators should invest more in lift infrastructure to improve visitor handling capacity.
Low-performing regions can increase marketing and tourism campaigns to attract visitors.
Dynamic pricing strategies can be implemented during peak winter seasons to maximize revenue.
Resorts should focus on customer experience improvements to maintain long-term growth.
